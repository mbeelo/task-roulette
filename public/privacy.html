Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 7, 2026

Introduction

Task Roulette ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our task management application.

Information We Collect

Local Storage Only: Task Roulette stores all your data locally in your browser's localStorage. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers.

The following data is stored locally on your device:

  • Task names and descriptions you create
  • Task metadata (difficulty, duration, frequency, category)
  • Task completion history and dates
  • Your app preferences (roulette mode, sort preferences)

How We Use Your Information

Your locally stored information is used solely to:

  • Display your tasks and preferences within the application
  • Maintain task completion history for daily recurring tasks
  • Remember your settings between sessions

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your data to third parties. Since all data is stored locally in your browser, only you have access to it.

Data Security

Your data security is ensured through browser localStorage security mechanisms. Your data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export it.

Data Retention

Your data is retained in your browser's localStorage until you:

  • Clear your browser's storage/cache
  • Manually delete tasks within the application
  • Uninstall or reset your browser

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally, you have complete control over your information:

  • View all your data within the application
  • Modify or delete any task or information
  • Export your data (if applicable)
  • Clear all data by clearing browser storage

Third-Party Services

Task Roulette does not integrate with or use any third-party tracking or analytics services. The application runs entirely in your browser without external data collection.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date above. Continued use of the application constitutes acceptance of any policy changes.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at our contact page.
